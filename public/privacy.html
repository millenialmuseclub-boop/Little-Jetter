Little Jetter Privacy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Little Jetter is a children’s travel dress-up game designed for ages 6–11. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising or child-to-child communication.

Information stored on the device

Outfits, saved looks, passport stamps, journal choices, parent settings, and saved shopping ideas are stored locally on the device. A grown-up can clear this information from Parent Review.

Information we do not request

The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone number, advertising identifier, or a child’s name, email address, or date of birth.

Parent shopping

Shopping and external retailer links are available only through the grown-up area. Retailer links may be affiliate links and may open an external website governed by that site’s privacy policy. Children should use this area with a parent or guardian.

Connections and third parties

When you use the website, Vercel receives normal connection information such as your IP address and requested files to serve the game. The game has no analytics or advertising SDK. Product images load from third-party image hosts only after a grown-up unlocks shopping and agrees to load retailer photos; these hosts receive normal connection information. Retailer websites may use cookies and affiliate tracking under their own policies.

Installed app and updates

The installed app bundles its play assets. A grown-up can request an optional update after agreeing to the notice in the grown-up area. The app then requests a static manifest and update files from Little Jetter’s Cloudflare file storage, which receives normal connection information such as an IP address and requested files. Stories, outfits, journal entries, generated device IDs, and advertising IDs are not sent. Background update checks are disabled. Device backups may be managed by the operating system.

Purchases

This build has no in-app purchases or subscriptions and no purchases to restore. Any physical goods are purchased separately from a retailer by a grown-up.

Developer and support

Little Jetter is a jordypop product. For privacy questions or support, visit the jordypop portfolio and contact page at jordypop.vercel.app (ask a grown-up to open it).
