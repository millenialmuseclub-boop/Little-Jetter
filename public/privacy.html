Little Jetter Privacy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Little Jetter is a children’s travel dress-up game designed for ages 6–11. The game does not require an account and does not include advertising or child-to-child communication.

Information stored on the device

Outfits, saved looks, passport stamps, journal choices, parent settings, and saved shopping ideas are stored locally on the device. A grown-up can clear this information from Parent Review.

Information we do not request

The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone number, advertising identifier, or a child’s name, email address, or date of birth.

Parent shopping

Shopping and external retailer links are available only through the grown-up area. Retailer links may be affiliate links and may open an external website governed by that site’s privacy policy. Children should use this area with a parent or guardian.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the Little Jetter support contact listed on its store page.
